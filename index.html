Troop control: drag to select troops; choose a commander for direct control.

Fan reconstruction · original game by Anthony Lopes / DarkSun Games

Four commanders. One army.

Select Commander 1–4 using the buttons below the battlefield. WASD controls your selected commander. Hold left-click to aim and fire continuously toward the cursor, including diagonally; right-click throws a grenade. The row above the commander selectors gives Normal, Follow, Attack and Defend orders to nearby troops. All four commanders use AI unless explicitly selected. Click the selected commander button again, or select troops, to return to troop control. Missions start in troop control. All bindings use physical key positions, so German QWERTZ works.

For the explicitly selected commander: physical W A S D moves, V fires and B chooses orders. Other commanders retain AI control.

Press Orders, then a direction: Up = Attack, Down = Defend, Right = Follow, Left = Normal. Fire + Orders throws a grenade. Walk over pickups and alien flowers. Commanders leave weapon pickups matching their current gun for other units; different guns still replace their equipment. Reinforcement eagles marked TROOPS FULL · WAIT FOR SPACE remain until troop capacity is available; SUPPORT UNAVAILABLE means the mission has no applicable support rule. Press Fire near a terminal or unlocked door to operate it. Drag with the left mouse button to select troops; right-click then attack-moves those troops. Attack-moving soldiers collect eagles close to their route and mount empty plasma turrets: eagles first in clear areas, turrets first when enemies are nearby. Mounted plasma cannons fire in 16 directions with either regular infantry or commando operators. Double right-click empty ground to force-move without stopping to fight; right-click an enemy or nest to focus attacks on it (double-click also works). Double right-click a door, terminal, pickup or usable objective to travel there and use it. Locked doors still require their terminal. Click a troop to select it; Shift adds to the selection. Click a commander button to return to mouse shooting and grenades. R or the top-right Rally button toggles rally placement. Left-click clear ground to place numbered flags; right-click a flag to remove it. Escape leaves rally mode. Ground, tank and Blitzkrieg soldiers/tanks/robots attack-move to the nearest reachable flag; commandos are excluded. Flags reset with each mission. Escape or Space toggles tactical mode; F2 restarts. Missions deploy into tactical mode with a blue-green visor. While tactical mode is active, simulation freezes and the battlefield stays visible: select troops and commanders, give squad orders, and issue attack-move, force-move, focus attacks or interaction orders. Units carry them out when you exit tactical mode.

The sprites, map backgrounds, object placements and sounds were recovered from your installed original. The engine, AI and mission logic are reconstructed and still differ from the original. Commander 4’s keyboard preset is an addition.
