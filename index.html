Reinforcement composition

Choose troops from this mission’s world roster. Saved settings apply to future matching eagle requests until changed. Inbound deliveries keep their original troops.

Ground squads have five slots. Air uses a repeating five-slot pattern along its flight path; actual arrivals depend on flight conditions and available troop capacity.

Floating Habitats opt-in: Mobile Skirmisher fires a 160 px light rifle for 1 damage every 0.75 seconds while continuing movement. Shooting never bends its route or Follow into pursuit; explicit Attack/focus may approach enemies. Platform Defender sets up after 1 second stationary, fires 300 px shots for 2 damage every 1.5 seconds, and packs for 0.5 seconds before deployed movement. It cannot move or fire during packing, or fire during travel/setup. Ordinary attack-move holds its destination while stopping to deploy against a legal contact, then packs and resumes when that contact ends. Interrupted partial setup cancels without packing; Guard reaches its anchor before deployment. Both have 1 HP, ordinary ground collision and eight-direction aiming; no gap jumps or immunity. Force/object use suppress firing. Fixed kits share troop capacity, terminal/door/support/grenade use; no weapon pickups/cannons. Tactical mode freezes all clocks. No Floating Habitats runtime mission is shipped.

Airless Moon opt-in: Heavy Trooper has 3 HP, 65% infantry movement and a 220 px rifle with 0.75-second shot interval. Select a Drone Operator to order its one 1 HP ground combat drone. Both share a single troop-cap slot, including pending landings. Drone follows movement, focus, Follow and Defend within 180 px; a blocked companion can hold back Operator movement. Force-move and object-use travel suppress drone firing. Drone cannot be selected independently, collect supplies or mount cannons. Destroyed drone is lost for this mission; Operator death removes it. Fixed kits use ordinary terrain and eight-direction aiming. No Airless Moon runtime mission is shipped.

Toxic Marsh opt-in Tracker marks visible enemies within 220 px every three seconds for two seconds. Nearby friendly AI uses only legal weapon targets; focus, force-move, Follow and Defend keep their rules. Chemical Trooper sprays a 100 px cone every 1.25 seconds, applying one enemy dose for 1 damage after one second. Further applications refresh delay and ownership; no cloud or stacking. Both share fixed infantry kits and orders. No Toxic Marsh runtime mission is shipped.

Volcanic Demolition Troopers: select troops, click Place charge, then left-click a reachable ground point. Right-click or Escape cancels placement mode. Normal orders cancel unfinished planting without spending a charge. Three finite charges per trooper; amber pips show remaining charges. Tactical mode queues placement until resumed. Cooling Troopers fire a 130 px cone for one second, then recharge for two; hits slow movement by 25 percent for 1.5 seconds.

Troops scan immediately and favor reachable firing lanes. Attack-move fights at weapon reach; short-range troops close before aligning. Defend may step within 50 pixels of its anchor and returns when clear. Bugs share local visible alarms within 120 pixels and investigate clear gunfire within 220 pixels; pursuit includes occasional breaks. Force-move, focus and use orders retain priority.

Standard gamepads: bumpers select a commander and claim one controller. Left stick moves; right stick aims freely; right trigger fires; left trigger throws one grenade per press; A interacts. D-pad: Up Attack, Down Defend, Left Normal, Right Follow. Start toggles tactical mode. Release sticks and buttons after selection, tactical mode, dialogs or focus loss to rearm. Other controllers stay inactive. Keyboard and mouse remain available.

2. Four commanders. One army.

Select Commander 1–4 using the buttons below the battlefield. WASD controls your selected commander. Hold left-click to aim and fire continuously toward the cursor, including diagonally; right-click throws a grenade. The row above the commander selectors gives Normal, Follow, Attack and Defend orders to nearby troops. All four commanders use AI unless explicitly selected. Click the selected commander button again, or select troops, to return to troop control. Missions start in troop control. All bindings use physical key positions, so German QWERTZ works.

For the explicitly selected commander: physical W A S D moves, V fires and B chooses orders. Other commanders retain AI control.

Press Orders, then a direction: Up = Attack, Down = Defend, Right = Follow, Left = Normal. Fire + Orders throws a grenade. Walk over pickups and alien flowers. Normal nests roll a 4% breeding opportunity every half second and take 1.8 seconds to emit a bug during a finite 2.4-second birth phase. Other original-mission settings use the same birth phase with 2% / 3% / 5% / 6% opportunities on Very easy / Easy / Hard / Very hard. Easier settings reduce nest cleanup and mandatory wave kills; harder settings add breeding fronts and pressure while keeping Normal wave kill targets. Normal remains the reference profile. Custom missions retain their own profiles. Normal growplants can regenerate in missions 1–3; destroy them before bugs evolve. Changing difficulty restarts the mission. Custom Relay Breaker requires activating both relay stations and clearing all nests and enemies; its merits are separate from the original campaign. All Relay profiles have four breeding nests and finite flank arrivals (Normal: 22; Hard: 33; Very hard: 44). Hard/Very hard reduce the starting army, remove tank support and use custom nest birth intervals of 2.6/2 seconds, compared with 4.5 seconds on Normal. Last Convoy Hard/Very hard also reduce the starting army and remove tank support. Last Convoy requires 120 seconds, all scheduled arrivals emitted (Normal: 26; Hard: 80; Very hard: 120) and every enemy cleared with a surviving noncommander. Losing the last soldier or robot causes immediate defeat, including while support is inbound. Silent Return requires using access terminal 253 to unlock door 252, opening it to activate laser terminal 257, then returning every living ground human within 96 pixels of (392,72), including delivered support. Keep a noncommander alive and wait for all support flights and drops to finish; enemies may remain. Beneath the Dunes: repair either disabled 2/6 HP crawler to full health with a mechanic, then keep a living ground escort within 110 pixels as it follows its crescent to extraction. Both destroyed, or no surviving mechanic before any living crawler can launch, loses. Four automatic mines per crawler; no immunity during evasion. Two finite yellow eagles on the north/south escort routes provide infantry by default: commanders collect them, soldiers can escort but cannot repair. Custom eagle supplies never refill; full infantry cap including Snow/Capital/Maritime/Undercity specialists and pending landing drops retains infantry support eagles. Missions 7–9 have ceiling shadows that cross walls and drop beside commanders or infantry; attack the bugs after they land. Ordinary bugs can return to vents in missions 7–8. Commanders leave weapon pickups matching their current gun for other units; different guns still replace their equipment. Open Reinforcements during a mission to configure five ground slots and a separate repeating five-slot air pattern from this world’s roster. Save applies to future matching eagles until changed; inbound deliveries retain their troops. Flight conditions and free troop capacity determine actual arrivals. Opening pauses play and closing automatically resumes, including when opened in tactical mode. Restart or mission change clears the settings. Reinforcement eagles marked TROOPS FULL · WAIT FOR SPACE remain until troop capacity is available; SUPPORT UNAVAILABLE means the mission has no applicable support rule. Press Fire near a terminal or unlocked door to operate it. Drag with the left mouse button to select troops; right-click then attack-moves those troops. Attack-moves soldiers collect eagles close to their route and mount empty plasma turrets: eagles first in clear areas, turrets first when enemies are nearby. Mounted plasma cannons fire in 16 directions with either regular infantry or commando operators. Snow Winter Gunners share troop orders but need 0.5 seconds standing still to set up; moving interrupts their burst. Their fixed kit cannot collect weapon pickups or mount cannons. Capital Shield Troopers and Laser Cannons also use fixed kits and shared orders. Shields protect only the bearer in a frontal 120-degree arc with 3 HP; all hits reset six-second recharge, overflow/side/rear/environmental damage reaches the body. They move at 75% infantry speed. Laser Cannon fires an eight-direction 220px, 8px-wide beam for 3 damage per enemy once, recharges four seconds and stops at terrain; allies are immune. Capital reinforcement menus offer both; Winter Gunner stays Snow-only. Maritime Suppressor fires four rounds within 200px and reloads 1.5 seconds; hits stop ordinary enemy movement for one second, refresh without stacking, and leave attacks active. Nests are not stopped. Maritime Grenadier predicts visible ground groups within 240px, launching a terrain-colliding projectile for a 40px, 2-damage blast every three seconds; allies survive and terrain blocks blast paths. Both share troop orders and fixed kits. Harbor Watch replaces one soldier with each, preserving army size. Undercity Corner Ambushers need one second stationary, fire three rounds 0.1 seconds apart in a clear 120px lane, then reprepare for two seconds. Movement cancels setup and interrupts a partial burst into two-second reprepare. Defend holds its assigned position without chasing or sidestepping; Attack/focus/attack-move can align, Follow and force-move retain priority. Fixed kit excludes weapon pickups/cannon mounting; explicit terminal/door/eagle use works. Tunnels Breach replaces one soldier, preserving army size; Undercity reinforcement menus offer Ambushers. Jungle Recon replaces one Canopy soldier with a fixed rifle and legal automatic projectile/melee dash: up to 48px, 0.25s immunity and 3s cooldown. Terrain, closed doors and bounds block the swept path; no safe dash means the hit lands. It cannot fire during dash, resumes its orders and returns toward Guard anchor. Jungle menus offer Recon; Field Medic is deferred without health changes. Industrial opt-in Heavy Riveter moves at 75% infantry speed and fires slow 90 px/s projectiles for 2 damage within 180 px every 1.2 seconds. Arc Technician deals 1 damage within 110 px, then one clear jump to a different visible enemy within 40 px of the first, recharging 1.5 seconds. Both use fixed eight-direction kits, shared troop orders and terrain limits; no weapon pickups or cannon mounting. Assembly Plant replaces two soldiers with a Heavy Riveter and an Arc Technician, preserving army size; Industrial reinforcement menus offer both specialists. Mercenary opt-in Weapon Specialist switches between a 240px single shot and a 120px three-pellet spread in 0.6 seconds with a one-second mode lockout. Select Specialists and choose Preferred weapon mode (Auto, Single, Spread). Auto uses spread for nearby visible groups. Bounty Hunter holds a visible target within 260px; one second stationary tracking charges its next shot to 2 damage, then resets. Movement and lost sight cancel tracking. Both use shared fixed infantry kits; no rewards or weapon pickups/cannon mounting. Mercenary Outpost Strike replaces two soldiers with a Weapon Specialist and a Bounty Hunter, preserving army size; Mercenary reinforcement menus offer both specialists. Double right-click empty ground to force-move without stopping to fight; right-click an enemy or nest to focus attacks on it (double-click also works). Double right-click a door, terminal, pickup or usable objective to travel there and use it. Locked doors still require their terminal. Click a troop to select it; Shift adds to the selection. Click a commander button to return to mouse shooting and grenades. R or the top-right Rally button toggles rally placement. Left-click clear ground to place numbered flags; right-click a flag to remove it. Escape leaves rally mode. Ground, tank and Blitzkrieg soldiers/tanks/robots attack-move to the nearest reachable flag; commandos are excluded. Flags reset with each mission. Escape or Space toggles tactical mode; F2 restarts. Missions deploy into tactical mode with a blue-green visor. While tactical mode is active, simulation freezes and the battlefield stays visible: select troops and commanders, give squad orders, and issue attack-move, force-move, focus attacks or interaction orders. Units carry them out when you exit tactical mode.
