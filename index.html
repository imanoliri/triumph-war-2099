Select a commander: WASD · left-click fires · right-click throws a grenade.

Fan reconstruction · original game by Anthony Lopes / DarkSun Games

Four commanders. One army.

Select Commander 1–4 using the buttons below the battlefield. WASD controls your selected commander. Hold left-click to aim and fire continuously toward the cursor, including diagonally; right-click throws a grenade. The row above the commander selectors gives Normal, Follow, Attack and Defend orders to nearby troops. Computer teammates control 2 and 3 when they are not selected. All bindings use physical key positions, so German QWERTZ works.

Press Orders, then a direction: Up = Attack, Down = Defend, Right = Follow, Left = Normal. Fire + Orders throws a grenade. Walk over pickups and alien flowers. Press Fire near a terminal or unlocked door to operate it. Drag with the left mouse button to select troops; right-click then attack-moves those troops. Click a troop to select it; Shift adds to the selection. Click a commander button to return to mouse shooting and grenades. Escape or Space pauses; F2 restarts.

The sprites, map backgrounds, object placements and sounds were recovered from your installed original. The engine, AI and mission logic are reconstructed and still differ from the original. Commander 4’s keyboard preset is an addition.
