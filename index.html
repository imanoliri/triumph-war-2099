Troop control: drag to select troops; choose a commander for direct control.

Fan reconstruction · original game by Anthony Lopes / DarkSun Games

Standard gamepads: bumpers select a commander and claim one controller. Left stick moves; right stick aims freely; right trigger fires; left trigger throws one grenade per press; A interacts. D-pad: Up Attack, Down Defend, Left Normal, Right Follow. Start toggles tactical mode. Release sticks and buttons after selection, tactical mode, dialogs or focus loss to rearm. Other controllers stay inactive. Keyboard and mouse remain available.

Four commanders. One army.

Select Commander 1–4 using the buttons below the battlefield. WASD controls your selected commander. Hold left-click to aim and fire continuously toward the cursor, including diagonally; right-click throws a grenade. The row above the commander selectors gives Normal, Follow, Attack and Defend orders to nearby troops. All four commanders use AI unless explicitly selected. Click the selected commander button again, or select troops, to return to troop control. Missions start in troop control. All bindings use physical key positions, so German QWERTZ works.

For the explicitly selected commander: physical W A S D moves, V fires and B chooses orders. Other commanders retain AI control.

Press Orders, then a direction: Up = Attack, Down = Defend, Right = Follow, Left = Normal. Fire + Orders throws a grenade. Walk over pickups and alien flowers. Normal nests roll a 4% breeding opportunity every half second and take 1.8 seconds to emit a bug during a finite 2.4-second birth phase. Other original-mission settings use the same birth phase with 2% / 3% / 5% / 6% opportunities on Very easy / Easy / Hard / Very hard. Easier settings reduce nest cleanup and mandatory wave kills; harder settings add breeding fronts and pressure while keeping Normal wave kill targets. Normal remains the reference profile. Custom missions retain their own profiles. Normal growplants can regenerate in missions 1–3; destroy them before bugs evolve. Changing difficulty restarts the mission. Custom Relay Breaker requires activating both relay stations and clearing all nests and enemies; its merits are separate from the original campaign. All Relay profiles have four breeding nests and finite flank arrivals (Normal: 22; Hard: 33; Very hard: 44). Hard/Very hard reduce the starting army, remove tank support and use custom nest birth intervals of 2.6/2 seconds, compared with 4.5 seconds on Normal. Last Convoy Hard/Very hard also reduce the starting army and remove tank support. Last Convoy requires 120 seconds, all scheduled arrivals emitted (Normal: 26; Hard: 80; Very hard: 120) and every enemy cleared with a surviving noncommander. Losing the last soldier or robot causes immediate defeat, including while support is inbound. Silent Return requires using access terminal 253 to unlock door 252, opening it to activate laser terminal 257, then returning every living ground human within 96 pixels of (392,72), including delivered support. Keep a noncommander alive and wait for all support flights and drops to finish; enemies may remain. Beneath the Dunes: repair either disabled 2/6 HP crawler to full health with a mechanic, then keep a living ground escort within 110 pixels as it follows its crescent to extraction. Both destroyed, or no surviving mechanic before any living crawler can launch, loses. Four automatic mines per crawler; no immunity during evasion. Missions 7–9 have ceiling shadows that cross walls and drop beside commanders or infantry; attack the bugs after they land. Ordinary bugs can return to vents in missions 7–8. Commanders leave weapon pickups matching their current gun for other units; different guns still replace their equipment. Reinforcement eagles marked TROOPS FULL · WAIT FOR SPACE remain until troop capacity is available; SUPPORT UNAVAILABLE means the mission has no applicable support rule. Press Fire near a terminal or unlocked door to operate it. Drag with the left mouse button to select troops; right-click then attack-moves those troops. Attack-moving soldiers collect eagles close to their route and mount empty plasma turrets: eagles first in clear areas, turrets first when enemies are nearby. Mounted plasma cannons fire in 16 directions with either regular infantry or commando operators. Double right-click empty ground to force-move without stopping to fight; right-click an enemy or nest to focus attacks on it (double-click also works). Double right-click a door, terminal, pickup or usable objective to travel there and use it. Locked doors still require their terminal. Click a troop to select it; Shift adds to the selection. Click a commander button to return to mouse shooting and grenades. R or the top-right Rally button toggles rally placement. Left-click clear ground to place numbered flags; right-click a flag to remove it. Escape leaves rally mode. Ground, tank and Blitzkrieg soldiers/tanks/robots attack-move to the nearest reachable flag; commandos are excluded. Flags reset with each mission. Escape or Space toggles tactical mode; F2 restarts. Missions deploy into tactical mode with a blue-green visor. While tactical mode is active, simulation freezes and the battlefield stays visible: select troops and commanders, give squad orders, and issue attack-move, force-move, focus attacks or interaction orders. Units carry them out when you exit tactical mode.

The sprites, map backgrounds, object placements and sounds were recovered from your installed original. The engine, AI and mission logic are reconstructed and still differ from the original. Commander 4’s keyboard preset is an addition.
