Authoring guide

Choose a generic layout first, then a theme/art reference. Select legal current-world kits and pressure roles. Give each objective a reachable approach and withdrawal, each support cache a finite budget, and each wave a population reservation. Check terrain footprints, firing lanes and the slowest unit’s response before tuning difficulty.

All 39 TRI-095 and six earlier Desert alternatives are retained by reference. Abandoned A is selected and implemented; Orbital B is selected with mission integration pending. A schematic, graph, VM result or diagram does not prove live rendering, audio or playability.

Eight-direction infantry and keyboard/AI commanders preserve aim constraints; mouse-selected commander and Snow Sniper aim freely; mounted cannon has its own 16 visual headings/sweep. Walls block sight/shots/movement; no art implies hazard immunity, gap jumping, flying drones, wall destruction or cross-world roster unlocking. Enemy-only container blast/fire and ordinary 1 HP infantry health remain fixed.

Health/weapon timing is contextual: Normal guide snapshot plus reinforcement factory evidence; original/custom profile enemies can differ. Projectile travel, target detection and engagement distance are separate. Force/object-use orders may suppress abilities. Current worldRoster, finite kit ammo, support caps and pending drops remain authoritative.
