# Custom mission candidates — TRI-023

These are proposed custom scenarios, not recovered original missions. Exact placements and proposed metadata are in [custom-missions.json](custom-missions.json). Relay Breaker is registered in the runtime under TRI-024; Last Convoy is registered under TRI-025; Silent Return remains a proposal. The director selected three candidates as working scope under the user's creation request; the user's count preference is still unsubmitted, not an answer or a blocker to design review. Start with **Relay Breaker**, viable on its own; later implementation tickets follow director approval and any user steering. A five-mission campaign would require two further designs, not duplicated variants claimed complete here.

Last Convoy and the Silent Return concept reuse recovered map art/masks; Relay Breaker now uses custom Split Ridge art/mask (TRI-032). All use existing unit, nest, terminal, weapon and eagle sprites. Active coordinates are sprite hotspots in the 1024×768 world; backdrop coordinates remain top-left. For recovered-terrain scenarios retain the chosen map's doors, breakable walls, barrels and cannons, but replace original humans, enemies, nests, flowers, pickups, waves and objectives with explicit custom lists. Disable original random pickup/growplant creation for these candidates; placed support remains available. That custom scenario policy must never alter the recovered nine maps. All start in tactical mode and use existing German physical-key controls, asymmetric aim, damage, caps and commander return rules.

## Relay Breaker — assault

**Brief:** Two alien breeding pockets are using the rocky relay corridor. Activate both relay stations and destroy the nests before the next convoy arrives.

Use custom Split Ridge (`assets/custom/split-ridge/terrain.png`) with Desert Rocks as the rules/support template. Deploy four commanders and eight soldiers on the western open strip; there is no initial tank. Capture relay sprites 257 at (800,200) and (800,560), in either order, and clear both nests plus every living ground/transition enemy. No minimum-time gate or waves. Two flags plus enemy clearance are required; killing enemies alone cannot win.

The northern approach runs through (128,160) to (800,160); the southern flank runs through (128,600) to (800,600). An eastern connector at x800 links both fronts. Nests at (904,240) and (904,600) and six placed bugs screen the pockets. Yellow support (224,576), bronze support (576,600) and plasma (352,176) are explicit. Neither support collection nor a perfect loot roll is mandatory for objective access.

Normal uses the existing finite birth phases for these two nests, with the existing ordinary-bug cap and cancellation on destruction. Other difficulties keep their existing profiles. Destroying nests, rather than waiting indefinitely, stops their pressure. Defeat uses existing loss of all commanders/army with no incoming support. Split Ridge is custom-authored scenery; no bespoke balance rule.

**Implementation acceptance:** independent relay keys despite the same sprite object ID; both activation orders work; missing either flag prevents victory; nests/births/ground enemies each prevent premature victory; destroying a busy nest cancels its birth; tactical freeze and restart reset all progress; troop/bronze support retains source map eligibility, caps and rally behavior. Live Normal playthrough must reach both stations and finish without geometry/LOS softlocks; record completion time, support use, casualties and remaining births before considering balance changes.

## Last Convoy — defense

**Brief:** The convoy needs two minutes to cross the base. Keep a fighting detachment alive, use the gun emplacements and clear the last attackers once the convoy is safe.

Reuse Hold Base (`assets/maps/13.png`, selector mission 5). Four commanders, ten soldiers and one robot deploy inside the base. Keep all five existing cannon positions: (330,615), (781,675), (451,300), (569,509), (150,194). There are four initial bugs, no nests or flowers, and **26 scheduled ordinary ground arrivals**: six at 20 seconds, eight at 50, twelve at 80. Arrival points alternate the northwest (256,208), north (584,200), east (840,520) and southeast (776,648). Do not retain the source mission's 200-kill replenishing wave.

Waves have a finite emission budget, separate from kills. Emit at most one scheduled bug each 0.5 seconds and queue any blocked by the ordinary cap; victory requires every scheduled emission to be consumed, 120 elapsed seconds, enemy clearance and at least one living noncommander. Loss of the last noncommander loses immediately; commander respawn alone cannot hold the convoy. Yellow support (520,584) and bronze support (584,648) offer a choice between replacing infantry and strengthening a flank. Retained cannon/door geometry offers interior and exterior routes; the fixture proves each cannon has a reachable approach, not that all positions have equal tactical value.

**Implementation acceptance:** exactly 26 scheduled emissions across restart/large dt/cap pressure; no reinforcements at tactical time; no victory at 119.9 seconds or while queued/ground bugs remain; loss when the final soldier/robot/tank dies, including while support is inbound; no original 200-kill quota; all five cannons mount/dismount and retain 16-heading fire; deterministic scenario logs record waves and surviving army. Live playtest must include one run holding two flanks, one loss run and cleanup of the last attacker. Timings/counts are proposed starting values, not proven balance.

## Silent Return — infiltration

**Brief:** Enter the old station with a small commando team. Unlock the laser room, disable its terminal and bring the surviving team back to the northern rendezvous. Avoiding a fight can be better than clearing the entire site.

Reuse Flash Back (`assets/maps/9.png`, selector mission 3). Four commanders and four commandos start in the north; there are six placed ground bugs, no nests, flowers, waves or ceiling actors. Use existing access terminal 253 at (480,617) from clear approach (464,617). This sets `used`, not `active`, and legitimately unlocks door 252. Walk/open the door, then activate terminal 257 at (844,270) from (836,262). An optional blue eagle at (520,648) calls existing outdoor air support; plasma at (520,520) is the only placed weapon.

Both terminal requirements must be satisfied before extraction can complete. Return every **currently living ground human**, including support arrivals, within 96 px of rendezvous (392,72), with at least one noncommander alive and no pending reinforcement/drop actors. Missing/dead commanders need not respawn before extraction. Existing enemies may remain; explicitly replace clear-all completion for this custom mission. Defeat follows ordinary total army/commander/support exhaustion. This is a small-squad raid with avoidance and return, not a stealth detection system. Commandos' eight-direction fire remains unchanged; no alarms, hiding or enemy-awareness rewrite.

Door 244 remains an unlocked optional southern exit. The fixture establishes the laser approach is unreachable with door 252 locked, the access terminal is reachable first, its real interaction unlocks 252, and the laser approach/return are then reachable under human openable-door planning. This does not claim a live walk through a physically closed door. Existing use/movement handles opening; the follow-up must exercise it.

**Implementation acceptance:** cannot enter the locked laser room before access; using 253 alone cannot win; laser activation alone cannot win; extraction rejects units outside radius, zero noncommanders or inbound support; enemies alone do not block extraction; force/use orders traverse the unlock/open sequence physically; tactical freeze/restart reset access/extraction; a live run demonstrates a legal outbound and return route without killing every bug.

## Runtime seam and bounded follow-up tickets

Current `begin`/`originalBegin`, nine-element `missions`, selector, public `loadMission(1..9)`, keyboard progression and victory text assume original indices. Wave spawn edges and crystal extraction also branch on `s.level`; support/pickup/vent rules key on `s.originalMap.index`. Simply appending missions would index absent original maps and inherit inappropriate requirements.

1. **Custom mission registry and Relay Breaker.** Add a dependency-free classic-script registry (e.g. `src/custom-missions.js`) with stable string IDs, explicit base map, placement lists, support policy and progress rules. Split terrain/source-template construction from scenario initialization only as far as this mission requires. Keep `s.level` and `loadMission(1..9)` meanings for originals; add custom identity and separate `loadCustomMission(id)`. Custom progress overrides must feed the same HUD/automatic victory calculation. Unique terminal instance keys must drive custom flags; object IDs still select sprites. No writes to `assets/original-*` or generated masks. Add separate Original / Custom selector groups and correct briefing/victory/progression text, so a custom win never accidentally advances an original level or reports original-campaign completion. Preserve original scoring/results; keep custom merits/progression separate and in memory. Add new script to index, loopback allowlist, package allowlist and syntax/runtime checks. Verify all nine originals before/after, tactics, Normal nest lifecycle, support rules, controls and custom restart/change/difficulty. Do not implement defense/infiltration in this first ticket.
2. **Last Convoy.** Add only this metadata, finite capped emission queue and its survival/time objective. Avoid changing source wave logic; regression-check original Hold Base and Crystal Chamber quotas. Requires the first ticket and approved mission count.
3. **Silent Return.** Add only this metadata and use/activate/return objective. Check real door unlock/open routes and support-aware extraction, plus original Flash Back's terminal/time/clear-all semantics. Requires the first ticket and approved count.

Proposed grouping is independent custom selection with no forced unlock chain; recommend assault → defense → infiltration as presentation order for the three-candidate working scope. Persistent saves, campaign economy, new art/maps, global balance changes, original-game research and remote publication are outside these tickets. The director owns count steering and any requested campaign locking before approving the affected implementation scope.

## Feasibility evidence and limitations

Run `node tools/check-custom-mission-design.cjs`. It borrows only the disposable VM bootstrap from the existing recreation test; its assertions are scenario feasibility diagnostics, not production mission behavior tests. It loads tracked maps and current dynamic geometry, removes original scenario actors in memory and validates 88 proposed placement/route entries with ±8 px corner clearance and connected 16 px human routes, 18 reachable cardinal firing lanes, required terminal interaction radius/order, five cannon approaches and source support eligibility. Indoor robot start uses the existing robot actor/sprite, not a new asset. Pending locked rooms are checked after their real terminal unlock; planning can traverse unlocked closed doors. Body clearance does not prove tank footprint or spawn formation spacing, which follow-up movement tests must establish.

All three designs are mechanically different and geometrically feasible with the current assets. Simulation geometry does not prove visual composition, camera/HUD readability, delivered support routes, difficulty, feel or live playability. Browser access remains unavailable in this run; no playtest is claimed. Metadata and values remain proposals until implementation and live validation. Count preference remains unsubmitted; the director's three-candidate working scope is recorded separately from a user answer.
