# Desert Rider roster (TRI-040)

Custom-authored opt-in mechanics in `src/desert-riders.js`; original missions and earlier custom mission placements remain unchanged. Beneath the Dunes (TRI-036) calls the internal `spawnDesertRider(type,x,y,route)` on the approved Twin Crescent map. The crawler follows an immutable copied waypoint route and is not player-selectable. New infantry uses the existing troop controller for selection, Normal/Follow/Attack/Defend, attack-move/force-move, focus, doors and terminals. Fixed on-foot equipment deliberately excludes weapon/eagle pickups, automatic supply trips and cannon mounting. Existing troops keep their own rules.

| Type | HP | Authored rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rider-scout` | 1 | Cardinal rifle: 1 damage, 0.6 s cooldown, 180 px sight. Visible ground bugs within 62 px trigger a 38 px retreat at 100 px/s, then 2.5 s rest. Nearby exposed worms qualify; a locked lane makes the scout sidestep perpendicular to it. Underground worms remain unseen. |
| `dune-guard` | 1 | Cardinal shotgun: exactly five 1-damage pellets, evenly spaced across 30 degrees, every 1.1 s, 290 px/s and 120 px projectile travel. Existing legal cardinal ray gate prevents diagonal wasted shots; Attack/focus can move into the legal lane. |
| `field-mechanic` | 1 | Same cardinal rifle cadence/sight as scout. Repairs one visible living damaged tank/ground robot/crawler within 62 px at 0.5 HP/s. Caps match actual spawn health: tank 8, robot 7, crawler 6; explicit `maxHP` overrides those defaults. No resurrection or infantry healing. |
| `convoy-crawler` | 6 | Normal route travel 30 px/s, square body extending 17 px per axis. Locked warning/charge lanes trigger at most one counter per telegraph identity. A clear diagonal destination changes both axes by exactly 32 px, traveled at 100 px/s. Four possible diagonals are tried in deterministic order; every intermediate full-body position must be clear. Completed counter rests 2 s and attempts one mine at the departure point. |

The crawler reacts during the 1.2 s locked warning, completing its 45.25 px diagonal in about 0.453 s in clear terrain. This matches Beneath Dunes proposal assumptions (17 px body / 32 px axes). Shared navigation adds its established 4 px conservative sample padding; pathfinding therefore prefers slightly wider corridors than the physical body alone. Unlocked doors are traversable in route planning and open on approach; physical motion still respects closed/locked doors. Stable per-radius blockers preserve navigation caches. Evasion sweeps never admit closed doors, rocks or map edges. No shield or damage immunity is granted: blocked/late counters remain vulnerable; a committed lane cannot trigger an endless repeated dodge.

Each crawler carries four nonrefilling mines. A completed evade consumes ammo only after valid placement at the clear departure point, at least 12 px from another mine. Mine body clearance is 4 px; arming takes 0.65 s; lifetime is 30 s. Exposed ground aliens trigger within 22 px. Blast deals 4 damage within 38 px with clear sight; terrain blocks blast. Mines never damage humans, underground worms, airborne units or walls/props. Death does not erase an already planted mine; expiry and terrain validation still apply. Barrel behavior remains enemy-only.

Visual provenance: the module draws small original canvas silhouettes and eight-pixel mine marks, separate from recovered sprite assets. No recovered assets were regenerated or altered. `tools/rider-sandbox.html` and `node tools/rider-preview.cjs` provide an isolated loopback demonstration; pellets there are emitted directly from the production pellet factory, while the actual shared infantry controller is verified in runtime VM tests. No mission challenge calibration or original-game research is claimed.
