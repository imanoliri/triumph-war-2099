## Player experience

Expedition command. Reach a stranded research station, activate its distress relay, and bring the surviving rescue party back to the extraction area.

## Proposed map

Two traversable approaches around an ice ridge: a short exposed station route and a longer route with a defensible regroup point. Prepare schematic alternatives for review; dimensions, mask, placements, pickups and support corridors are not frozen.

## Objective contract to refine

Relay activated, designated rescued units acquired by an approved rule, and required living survivors inside extraction; exact eligibility and army exhaustion rules need agreement. Decide timeout, defeated rescue targets, commander eligibility, reinforcement cap and extraction arrivals explicitly.

## Dependencies and exclusions

Existing humans stand in for the stranded force in the first version. A civilian rescue system and ice-burrowing enemy are separate dependencies. No campaign persistence, new environmental damage or unselected faction abilities are authorized by this brief.
