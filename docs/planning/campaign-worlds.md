# Campaign, place and faction brainstorm

Recorded2026-10-05; planning only. No campaign/unit/runtime implementation authorized.

## User direction

Hybrid campaign: continuing expedition story with occasional missions commanding local factions. Each place may have its own missions, independent colonies/settlements, human variants, environmental quirks and adapted bugs. User examples: Navy, mercenaries, spec ops, Desert Riders, Sewer Rats; frontier worlds, expeditionary forces and rebels; burrowing/flying/worm/spider bugs. User explicitly added snowy planet, ocean/island maritime planet and capital urban planet, and wants more places than their initial examples.

## Director proposals for discussion (not agreed implementation)

A regional chapter can progress arrival, local contact, escalation, joint final operation. Local-command missions reveal routes/tactics and affect later allies/resources; expedition continuity connects chapters. Keep controls familiar, introduce one main friendly role and one main enemy adaptation at a time. Factions may recur across worlds with local equipment and goals. Core force persistence/recruitment/progression rules remain unrefined.

Place candidates: desert frontier, maritime islands/ports/drowned city, glacier/tundra/ice caves, undercity/sewers, jungle expedition, industrial rebel world, mercenary frontier, volcanic mines, toxic marsh, airless moon, floating habitats, overgrown abandoned enclaves, orbital scrapyard and capital metropolis. Capital districts can include government/communications, residences/courtyards/interiors, industrial rail/power, spaceport and undercity. Urban forces could include metropolitan guards, assault teams, rescue engineers and neighborhood militia; local guard defense followed by expedition relief is a hybrid mission example.

Possible counters: scouts reveal burrow warnings; demolition reopens webbed routes; Navy engineers manage crossings; local guides expose tunnel connections. Snow rescue/geothermal defense, island assault/harbor evacuation, foundry/cooling defense, marsh purification, dome repair, bridge/platform defense, reclamation escort and ship salvage are unapproved mission seeds. No stealth/physics/tides/hazard mechanics are implied as implemented. Separate future bounded refinement tickets when the user selects a chapter or role; maintain source/custom distinction and asymmetric aim/control conventions.
