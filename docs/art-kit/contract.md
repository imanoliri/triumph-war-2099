# Immutable art contract

Use `manifest.json` as the snapshot of approved geometry and file hashes, not the schematic silhouette. Current `assets/custom/split-ridge/collision.png` and the packed mask in `terrain.js` are authoritative. The three concept rectangles are outer envelopes; they are not replacement masks.

- Canvas/world: exactly 1024×768, origin upper left; x rightward, y downward. Preserve the quiet top 32 world pixels behind the live HUD. Do not draw HUD/text into terrain. Existing terrain uses a 512×384 authoring grid scaled nearest-neighbor ×2. Preserve that texture cadence and draw actors at their original native dimensions, never enlarged atlas scale.
- Collision: PNG black (0) is clear; white (255) blocked. Packed bytes contain 1024×768 bits, row-major `n=y*1024+x`, byte `n>>3`, low bit `1<<(n&7)` first; exactly 98,304 bytes. No alpha-derived collision or edited mask. Every bit must remain identical. `geometry.png` magenta is blocked, cyan hotspot marks are reference overlays only.
- Overlay labels: C commander, S soldier, B ordinary bug, N nest, R relay; troop/tank support and plasma are spelled out. Full exact points are in manifest.json.
- Keep all 25 hotspots and route geometry in the manifest. Sprite top-left is `(x-hx,y-hy)`, using the selected frame's metadata; hotspots are not centers/bounding-box corners. Full animation metadata remains in `assets/original-data.js`. Do not move/resize relays, nests, plasma, yellow troop/bronze tank support, start formations or support entry/drop paths.
- Preserve all clear corridor widths, route turns, nest firing approaches and support access. Existing compiled-mask checks use 18 px body clearance and real tank/squad circuits; decorative shadows do not create obstacles. Do not paint apparent impassable walls on clear routes, or apparently traversable sand over blocked rock. Collision-free scenery must remain sparse, low contrast and visually distinct from blocking cliffs and interactable objects.
- Allowed: sand grain/striations, cliff faces within the fixed rock shape, edge lighting and soft directional shadow on clear ground, sparse purely decorative ground details. Preserve tactical readability of commander/bug silhouettes, nest fronts, relay interaction points and support markers. Avoid brightly colored pickup-like decorations.
- Never bake actors, bugs, eggs/nests, pickups, terminals, cannons, damageable props, particles or HUD into terrain. Existing recovered sprite/object art is reference-only and unchanged. Do not copy a flattened original map or screenshot as new terrain.
- Obstacles/layout changes are a separate director/user decision before implementation; do not "improve" the mask while beautifying. No gameplay, aiming, German physical-key controls, support rules or enemy-only barrel damage changes.

Deliver custom-owned output under `assets/custom/<approved-art-slug>/` in the future art ticket, separate from recovered files and current runtime wiring: editable source (layered file or deterministic script/SVG plus seed and build command), `terrain.png`, provenance/change notes, at-scale before/after and actor-overlay comparison, explicit baseline/current SHA-256 evidence for collision PNG and terrain.js and byte-level mask comparison. List any generated pixels/source-derived pixels separately. Terrain absence of live entities requires visual inspection of editable layers and final raster; hashes alone cannot prove that. Record image-generation prompt/tool/skill when used. Stop at Review with exact paths, commit and checks; runtime installation belongs only to explicitly approved scope.
